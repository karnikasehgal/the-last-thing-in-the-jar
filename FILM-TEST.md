# Video test: Mikon's wedding in Hailuo

The goal is four short clips, so the video version can be judged against the 3D one before anything else changes.

## Setup in Hailuo (MiniMax)

- Use **image to video** with a subject reference where it's offered. Upload Mikon's Canva design (MAHX42SAltw) as the reference, so he looks the same in every clip.
- 16:9, 1080p, the longest clip length available (6 or 10 seconds).
- Paste the **style line** at the start of every prompt. It holds the clips together.
- Make 2 or 3 takes of each and keep the best one. Bad hands and faces are normal; reroll them.

**Style line**

> Stylised 3D animated feature film, Disney/Pixar quality, appealing cartoon characters with big expressive eyes, believable cloth and skin. Dark theatrical lighting: night, hundreds of white candles, black reflective water, ivory silk drapery, deep shadows, warm candle glow on faces, gentle haze. Palette of black, ivory, gold and a touch of deep red. Ancient Greece. No text, no captions, no logos.

## The four shots

Save each one with exactly this file name in `public/play/film/ch1/`.

**01.mp4: the wedding (establishing)**
> Slow crane shot descending into an ancient stone quarry at night, turned into a wedding banquet. A long table runs beside a still black pool down the middle, lined with hundreds of white candles and floating candles on the water. Sheer ivory silk falls in long drapes from the quarry walls. Guests in ivory, gold and black robes laugh and raise cups; a lyre plays. Reflections of the candles shimmer in the water.

**02.mp4: Mikon arrives**
> Mikon, a stocky, round-bellied Greek man in his forties with curly black hair, stubble and a gap-toothed grin, in an oatmeal wool tunic with a rope belt, steps into the candlelit quarry banquet, beaming, hugging a wicker basket of salt fish like treasure. He waves at someone off screen. Medium shot, candlelight on his face, black pool and white candles behind him.

**12.mp4: the olive**
> Close on Mikon at the candlelit banquet table, laughing hard at his own joke, head thrown back. Mid-laugh he gasps, freezes, eyes going wide, and clutches his throat. Comic timing turning to alarm. Candles and ivory drapes softly out of focus behind him.

**28.mp4: the Styx**
> Huge pull-back crane shot at night: Mikon sits small and alone on a dark beach of black sand beside a wide still river that glows with soft blue bioluminescence where it touches the shore. A long wooden dock reaches into mist, a black ferry waits at the end with one lantern. Thousands of faint pale drifting lights over the water. No palm trees.

## Watching it in the game

With the dev server running, open:

`http://localhost:8790/play/?chapter=1&clips`

Choose Chapter I, Enter, Begin. Whatever clips are in the folder play in order with subtitles, then the game starts. Esc or the skip button jumps ahead.

The clips carry their own sound. The game doesn't add voices on top of video yet, so the lines show as subtitles only.
