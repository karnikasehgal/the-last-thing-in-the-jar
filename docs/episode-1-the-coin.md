# The Last Thing in the Jar
## Episode I: The Coin

*The bank of the River Styx. One hundred years. One man without a coin.*

**Running time:** about 10–12 minutes. A new painting every 2–3 seconds. Every speaking character has their own voice and is shown as a painted face when they talk.

**The rule (from the sources):** the Greeks buried their dead with a small coin, an obol, to pay Charon the ferryman. Virgil writes that the dead who aren't properly buried must wander the riverbank for a hundred years before they can cross. *(Aristophanes, Frogs; Virgil, Aeneid 6.325–330)* We combine the two: no coin, no boat, a hundred years on the bank.

**Our protagonist is in the sources, sort of.** Apuleius tells how Psyche was sent to the Underworld with two coins in her mouth, and warned that a dead old man would raise his hands from the river and beg her to take him on the boat, and that she must not feel sorry for him. *(Apuleius, Metamorphoses 6.18–19)* That's all we ever hear about him. This is his story.

---

**Cast**
- **MIKON.** Died young, stupidly, at his cousin's wedding. No coin. Not brave, not clever, very polite. Talks to himself a lot.
- **HERMES.** Guide of the dead. Drops souls off at the river like a man with a lot of stops.
- **CHARON.** The ferryman. Ancient. Says one word, usually "Coin."
- **ORPHEUS.** The greatest singer who ever lived, here to get his wife back.
- **THESEUS and PIRITHOUS.** Two heroes on a terrible plan: kidnapping the Queen of the Underworld so Pirithous can marry her.
- **HERACLES.** Here for the dog.
- **CERBERUS.** The dog. Three heads.
- **ELPENOR.** The newest arrival. Fell off a roof. (A real character from the Odyssey.)
- **AENEAS and THE SIBYL.** A hero with a golden branch and a priestess who's in a hurry.
- **PSYCHE.** Two coins in her mouth. Strict instructions.

**The running obstacle:** the oar. Every time Charon pushes the boat off, the oar splashes Mikon. Every time. For a hundred years.

---

### COLD OPEN

*Black. The slap of water. Fast cuts: a dark river, a boat, an old man's face, a three-headed dog, a crowd of grey figures.*

**MIKON** *(close, breathless)*: Okay. Okay okay okay. I have it. I actually have it.

*Cut: a coin between two fingers.*

**MIKON**: Do NOT drop it. Do not drop the—

*A splash.*

**MIKON**: ...Every time.

**TITLE:** THE LAST THING IN THE JAR. *Episode I: The Coin.*

**CARD:** *One hundred years earlier.*

---

### SCENE 1: THE WEDDING

*A banquet. Music, dancing, food. Mikon at the back of the room.*

**MIKON**: My cousin's wedding. Great food. I'm on my fourth plate of olives, which is three more than I should be on.

**MIKON**: Here is a thing nobody tells you about olives.

*Cut: a funeral. A body laid out on a bier. Mourners.*

**MIKON**: They have pits.

---

### SCENE 2: THE BANK

*Grey light. A river. Hundreds of the dead, waiting. Hermes, with his winged hat, steering Mikon along by the elbow.*

**HERMES**: Right. Here's you. River's there, boat's there, ferryman's that one. Coin?

**MIKON**: Sorry?

**HERMES**: The coin. They put a coin in your mouth. For the fare.

**MIKON**: *(feeling around his mouth)* I think there was a wedding on.

**HERMES**: Ah. *(already leaving)* Hundred years, then. Rules are rules. Lovely to meet you.

*The boat. Charon, ancient and filthy, oar in hand. Mikon steps up.*

**MIKON**: Hi! Quick question about the—

**CHARON**: Coin.

**MIKON**: I don't have a—

*Charon pushes off. The oar slaps the water. Mikon is soaked.*

**MIKON**: Okay. That's fine. That's fine. Ninety-nine years and three hundred and sixty-four days.

> **CHOICE 1. A hundred years is a long time. What's the plan?**
> - **Make friends. You'll be here a while.** → **MIKON**: Hi, I'm Mikon. What are you in for? *(a shade turns, slowly)* Okay, we'll circle back.
> - **Start looking for a coin. They have to drop somewhere.** → **MIKON**: People die rich all the time. Somebody's going to drop one. *(He gets on his knees and starts searching the mud.)*
> - **Ask Charon nicely. Every day.** → **MIKON**: Morning, Charon! **CHARON**: Coin. **MIKON**: Tomorrow, then!

---

### SCENE 3: THE SINGER

*Years later. Music, out of nowhere. Every shade on the bank turns. A man with a lyre is walking down to the water.*

**MIKON**: Who is that.

**A SHADE**: Orpheus. His wife died. He's come to sing her back.

**MIKON**: Can you do that?

*Orpheus plays. Cut: Charon's face. Charon is crying.*

**MIKON** *(whispering)*: Charon's crying. I didn't know he had... water.

*The boat is loading. Everyone is too moved to notice anything.*

> **CHOICE 2. Charon is weeping. Nobody's watching the boat.**
> - **Sneak on.** → *Mikon creeps toward the boat. One foot on the edge. Charon, still crying, pushes off.* *(Splash.)*
> - **Ask Orpheus for a coin.** → **MIKON**: Sorry, huge fan, quick one, do you have a spare— **ORPHEUS** *(not stopping)*: I'm getting my wife. **MIKON**: Totally. Totally. Good luck.
> - **Sing along.** → *Mikon hums. Badly. Three shades move away from him.*

*Later. Orpheus comes back up the bank. Alone. He walks past Mikon without seeing him.*

**MIKON** *(quietly)*: He looked back. *(beat)* Yeah. I'd have looked back too.

---

### SCENE 4: THE CHAIRS

*Two big men stride down the bank, laughing. Armour, swagger.*

**THESEUS**: Down, in, grab the queen, out. Easy.

**MIKON**: Sorry, grab who?

**PIRITHOUS**: Persephone. I'm going to marry her.

**MIKON**: She's married. To Hades. Who is... the guy who owns all this.

**PIRITHOUS**: For now.

*They pay Charon. They cross. Mikon gets splashed. Cut: the throne room of the dead. Hades, enormous. He is very calm.*

**HADES**: Please. Sit. You must be tired.

*Two stone chairs.*

> **CHOICE 3. You can hear this from the bank. Do you shout a warning?**
> - **"DON'T SIT DOWN!"** → *They sit down.* **MIKON**: ...Okay.
> - **Stay out of it. They're heroes, they'll figure it out.** → *They sit down.* **MIKON**: ...Okay.
> - **"Is anyone else seeing this?"** → **A SHADE**: Yes. **MIKON**: Should we— **A SHADE**: No. *They sit down.*

**THESEUS** *(far away)*: I can't get up.

**PIRITHOUS**: Why can't I get up?

**MIKON**: Because you sat down in the house of the god of death!

---

### SCENE 5: THE DOG

*A sound like three dogs barking at once. Then a SUDDEN cut, with a hit on the soundtrack: Cerberus. Three heads. Teeth.*

**MIKON**: NOPE—

*Cut: Heracles, lion skin over his shoulders, walking straight at the dog.*

**HERACLES**: Who's a good boy. Who's a good, big, three-headed—

*He wrestles Cerberus. The ground shakes. Heracles shoves past the queue and drags Charon's boat to the shore himself.*

**CHARON**: Coin.

**HERACLES**: No.

*Charon looks at him for a long time. Then rows him across.*

**MIKON**: OH, so THAT works?

> **CHOICE 4. Heracles doesn't pay. Heracles just goes.**
> - **Try it yourself. "No."** → **MIKON** *(firmly)*: No. **CHARON**: Coin. **MIKON**: Okay, yeah, no, that's fair. *(Splash.)*
> - **Ask Heracles to take you with him.** → **HERACLES**: Can't, mate. Got a dog to carry. *(He's holding Cerberus under one arm. All three heads look at Mikon.)*
> - **Hide behind a rock until the dog is gone.** → *Mikon hides. A dog head appears over the rock. Then a second one.* **MIKON**: I'll hide behind a different rock.

*Heracles comes back the other way, carrying Cerberus, and Theseus, peeled off his chair. Pirithous is still sitting.*

**PIRITHOUS** *(far away)*: Guys?

---

### SCENE 6: THE NEW GUY

*Years pass. Quick cuts: the bank, the grey crowd, the boat leaving, splash, splash, splash. A young man stumbles down to the water, rubbing his neck.*

**ELPENOR**: Where am I.

**MIKON**: Dead! Welcome. How'd you go?

**ELPENOR**: Fell off a roof. I was asleep up there. Somebody shouted, I got up, I forgot I was on a roof.

**MIKON**: Olive pit.

*They nod at each other. Two professionals.*

**ELPENOR**: Coin?

**MIKON**: Nope.

**ELPENOR**: Me neither. My captain left before anyone buried me.

*Later. At the edge of the dark, a living man, Odysseus, kneels over a pit of blood, and the dead crowd around him. Elpenor runs to the front.*

**ELPENOR**: Odysseus! It's me! Go back and bury me! Put my oar on top so people know I was a rower!

*Odysseus nods. Elpenor turns to Mikon, glowing.*

**ELPENOR**: He's going to bury me! I'm getting out!

**MIKON**: That's great. That's great for you. *(beat)* Could you ask him if he has a coin?

*Elpenor is already gone.*

---

### SCENE 7: THE BRANCH

*Decades. Mikon is older now, grey, sitting in the shallows. A hero walks down with a priestess, holding a branch that glows gold.*

**THE SIBYL**: Keep walking. Don't make eye contact with the unburied, they'll talk to you.

**MIKON**: Hi! I'm unburied!

**THE SIBYL**: See.

*Aeneas holds up the golden branch. Charon stares at it, and lets them on.*

**MIKON** *(to no one)*: A coin. A branch. A song. A dog. Everybody's got something.

*Splash.*

---

### SCENE 8: THE GIRL WITH TWO COINS

*Ninety-nine years in. Mikon is an old man, waist-deep in the river, almost part of it. A young woman comes down to the boat, walking very carefully. Her cheeks are puffed out: she's holding two coins in her mouth.*

**MIKON**: Two. She's got TWO.

*He wades toward her, hands out of the water.*

**MIKON**: Miss! Miss! Sorry! Could you possibly—

*Psyche looks at him. Her eyes fill. She shakes her head, and keeps walking.*

**MIKON**: No, I get it. You're not allowed. Somebody told you not to. *(beat)* Somebody's always told you not to.

*Psyche, in the boat, looks back at him once. Splash.*

---

### SCENE 9: ONE HUNDRED YEARS

*Charon, on the bank, looking at Mikon. For the first time, he speaks a second word.*

**CHARON**: Time.

**MIKON**: Sorry?

**CHARON**: A hundred years. Get in.

*Mikon stands up. He looks at the boat. Then at the bank. A new arrival is there: a kid, frightened, with no coin.*

> **CHOICE 5. You can finally cross. There's a kid on the bank with no coin.**
> - **Get in the boat. A hundred years. You earned it.** → **MIKON** *(to the kid)*: It goes faster than you'd think. Make friends. Watch out for the oar.
> - **Give the kid your place.** → **CHARON**: Doesn't work like that. **MIKON**: Then I'll wait with him. **CHARON** *(long pause)*: ...Both of you. Get in.
> - **Stay. You've got a good spot now.** → **MIKON**: You know what? I've got a rock. I've got a view. I've seen Heracles. *(He sits back down.)* Next century.

*Whatever he chooses, the oar dips into the water, and for the first time in a hundred years, he doesn't get splashed. He's in the boat. Or he's watching it, dry, from his rock.*

**MIKON**: Huh. *(beat)* Could be worse.
