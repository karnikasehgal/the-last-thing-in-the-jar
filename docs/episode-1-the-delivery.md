# The Last Thing in the Jar
## Episode I: The Delivery

*Crete and Mount Othrys. The Golden Age. One night.*

**Running time:** about 10 minutes. **Cuts:** a new painting every 2–3 seconds. Every speaking character has their own voice and is shown as a painted face when they talk.

**Cast**
- **EUDOROS**, a courier. Gig worker in an age where nobody works. Mutters constantly. Means well. Not built for this.
- **RHEA**, queen of the Titans, mother of a newborn she is trying very hard to hide.
- **A CURETE**, an armed dancer outside a cave. Very committed to the bit.
- **AMALTHEA**, a goat.
- **THE GUARD**, a Titan on the door. Has kids.
- **CRONUS**, king of the Titans. Eats his children. Doesn't chew.

**What's from the myths:** Cronus swallowed his children to stop a prophecy coming true. Rhea gave birth to Zeus in Crete, hid him in a cave, and gave Cronus a stone wrapped like a baby, which he swallowed. Armed young men, the Curetes, clashed their weapons on their shields so Cronus wouldn't hear the baby cry. The baby was fed with the milk of Amalthea. Years later Zeus made Cronus bring the stone back up, and it was set up at Delphi. *(Hesiod, Theogony 453–500; Apollodorus 1.1.5–1.2.1; Pausanias 10.24.6)*

**What's invented:** the courier, and everything that happens to him.

---

### COLD OPEN

*Black. Breathing. Running footsteps. Quick cuts: a mountain at night, a hand gripping a bundle, a huge hall, an enormous painted face.*

**EUDOROS** *(running, muttering)*: Cold. Cold. Wet sandal. Why is it only ONE wet sandal. Why is the goat faster than me.

**EUDOROS**: Okay. In my defence? I asked if it was a baby.

**TITLE:** THE LAST THING IN THE JAR. *Episode I: The Delivery.*

**CARD:** *Six hours earlier.*

---

### SCENE 1: THE PICKUP

*Golden Age paintings: people lounging in sunshine, fruit falling into their hands.*

**EUDOROS**: The Golden Age. Nobody works. Nobody gets sick. Nobody gets old. Which means nobody needs anything. Which means nobody tips.

**EUDOROS**: Except me. I need things. I need to eat.

**EUDOROS**: One job tonight. Pickup in Lyktos, here in Crete. Drop-off at the king's hall on Mount Othrys. That's across a sea. Flat rate.

*A woman in a doorway. Rhea, frightened, holding a bundle.*

**RHEA**: You're the courier?

**EUDOROS**: Depends what needs couriering.

**RHEA**: My son. To his father. Tonight.

**EUDOROS**: Oh, that's nice. Family thing.

*She hands it over. Cut to his face.*

**EUDOROS**: Oof. Ma'am. That is a dense baby.

**RHEA**: He's big for his age.

**EUDOROS** *(under his breath)*: He's one day old.

**RHEA**: Into the king's hands. Nobody else's. Go.

> **CHOICE 1. The bundle is very, very heavy.**
> - **Ask what's really in it.** → **EUDOROS**: So what's actually in the— **RHEA**: A baby. **EUDOROS**: Great. Love that. Baby.
> - **Ask about the tip.** → **EUDOROS**: And the tip situation? **RHEA**: If he lives, you'll never work again. **EUDOROS**: If who lives? **RHEA**: GO.
> - **Say nothing. Rent's due.** → **EUDOROS**: I say nothing. Rent is due. Rent is always due.

---

### SCENE 2: THE CAVE

*A mountain road at night. Then: CLANG. CLANG. CLANG. Young men in armour, hammering weapons on shields outside a cave.*

**EUDOROS**: What is... that.

**CURETE**: Keep walking, friend.

**EUDOROS**: Is there a baby crying in there?

**CURETE**: No. This is dancing.

**EUDOROS**: You're hitting shields.

**CURETE**: Loud dancing.

> **CHOICE 2. There is definitely a baby crying in that cave.**
> - **Peek inside.** → **EUDOROS**: There's a baby in there. A real one. Being fed by a goat. *(beat, looks down at his bundle)* Oh, no. *(beat)* The goat is looking at me.
> - **Keep walking. Not your business.** → **EUDOROS**: Not my business. Not my business. Not my... why is there a goat. Why is the goat following me.
> - **Ask if they need anything delivered.** → **EUDOROS**: Shields? Earplugs? I do returns. **CURETE**: Walk.

*All three: the goat is now with him.*

**AMALTHEA**: Meeehh.

**EUDOROS**: No. No. Go home. You have a job. You have a baby to feed. *(it eats the corner of his delivery slip)* That was my paperwork.

*He steps into a stream.*

**EUDOROS**: Oh, come on.

---

### SCENE 3: THE CROSSING

*Night sea. A small boat. Quick cuts.*

**EUDOROS**: Golden Age. Perfect weather. Calm sea. Not one wave. And I'm still throwing up.

**EUDOROS**: The goat is fine. The goat loves boats.

---

### SCENE 4: THE DOOR

*Mount Othrys. Enormous figures: the Titans. Cut to a Titan guard, looming.*

**THE GUARD**: Business.

**EUDOROS**: Delivery for the king. Signature required.

**THE GUARD**: What's in it.

**EUDOROS**: His son.

**THE GUARD** *(leaning in)*: That baby looks weird.

> **CHOICE 3. The guard is staring at the bundle.**
> - **Straight face: "They're all lumpy at this age."** → **THE GUARD** *(slowly)*: ...Yeah. Mine was lumpy. Go in.
> - **Tell him the truth and hope he's a dad.** → **EUDOROS**: Honestly? I don't think it's a baby. **THE GUARD** *(long pause)*: I have four kids. I didn't hear that. Go in.
> - **Start crying. Proud uncle energy.** → **EUDOROS** *(sobbing)*: He's just so beautiful. **THE GUARD**: Please stop. Go in. Please.

---

### SCENE 5: THE KING

*A vast dark hall. Silence. Then a SUDDEN cut, with a deep hit on the soundtrack: Cronus's face, enormous.*

**CRONUS**: Give it.

**EUDOROS**: Hi. Yes. I just need a quick sign—

*A sound like a drain. Cut, cut, cut: paintings of Saturn devouring his children.*

**EUDOROS** *(whispering)*: He didn't chew.

**CRONUS**: You came from Crete.

**EUDOROS**: Did I?

**CRONUS**: Did you see anything on the road?

> **CHOICE 4. Cronus is waiting for an answer.**
> - **"Nothing. Some dancing."** → **CRONUS**: Dancing. **EUDOROS**: Loud dancing. Very loud. Young people.
> - **Blame the goat.** → **EUDOROS**: Honestly, sir, the goat's been my main issue. *(Cronus turns, slowly, to look at the goat. The goat looks back.)*
> - **"So that's a no on the signature?"** → **CRONUS** *(very long pause)*. *(He presses a huge thumb into the wet clay of Eudoros's slip.)* **EUDOROS**: Thank you. Have a great night.

**CRONUS**: Leave.

**EUDOROS**: Leaving. Happily. Gladly. Leaving.

*The goat eats a tapestry. A Titan roars.*

**EUDOROS**: NOPE.

---

### SCENE 6: THE RUN

*Back to the cold open. Fast cuts: giants, a mountain, night, the sea.*

**EUDOROS** *(running, muttering)*: Cold, cold, wet sandal, goat, giant, giant, goat, why is the goat FASTER than me, I'm a professional, I do this for a living...

---

### SCENE 7: THE TIP

*Dawn. Crete. Rhea in a doorway again.*

**RHEA**: Did he take it?

**EUDOROS**: Swallowed it. Whole. Didn't chew. That's not a parenting note, just an observation.

**RHEA**: You saw nothing tonight.

**EUDOROS**: I saw a lot tonight.

**RHEA**: You saw nothing.

**EUDOROS**: I saw nothing.

**RHEA**: *(handing him a single coin)*: There'll be more work. Quiet work. For years, maybe.

> **CHOICE 5. Rhea is offering steady work. In the Golden Age. Where nobody works.**
> - **Take it. Steady work is steady work.** → **EUDOROS**: Benefits? **RHEA**: No. **EUDOROS**: I'll take it.
> - **Turn it down. You've had enough of gods.** → **EUDOROS**: With respect, your family is a lot. **RHEA**: *(almost smiling)* Yes.
> - **Ask her what happens when the baby grows up.** → **RHEA**: He'll come back for his brothers and sisters. **EUDOROS**: From... inside? **RHEA**: From inside.

**EUDOROS** *(walking away, the goat beside him)*: One coin. One wet sandal. One goat. *(beat)* Could be worse.
