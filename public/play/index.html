How to get out of here

1 · Talk to peopleWalk up to someone and press E. Hermes is waiting for you at the start.
2 · Find a coinCharon won't take you across without one. The only coin is in the dog's cave. Ask the sailor by the river how to get it.
3 · Put the dog to sleepThrow him the honey cake from the temple altar. You'll have about 25 seconds.
4 · Run to the ferryTake the coin to Charon at the end of the dock. When the dog wakes he smells the coin and hunts you. He can't swim, so the river is safe.
HelpThe top of the screen always says what to do, with an arrow and a distance. Press M for this map.
